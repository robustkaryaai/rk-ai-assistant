# 🎙️ RK AI Assistant V2 — Alexa-Class Feature Catalog

> **Target Hardware**: Raspberry Pi Zero W (ARMv6, single-core 1GHz, 512MB RAM)  
> **Dedicated Audio Output**: Bluetooth Speaker (`D0:78:1D:4F:F4:1E`) on dedicated adapter (`hci1`)  
> **Architecture**: Ultra-lightweight local core + Cloud Intelligence (`RK_AI_HOME` + Gemini)  
> 
> *Instructions: Review this document and delete/edit any features you do not want.*

---

## 🎵 1. Audio, Music & Smart Playback (Alexa-Class)

- [ ] **1.1. Dynamic Audio Ducking & Barge-In Interrupt**
  - While music is playing, hearing the wake-word (*"rk"*) instantly lowers playback volume to 15% instead of killing it.
  - Users can interrupt the assistant mid-speech or mid-song by saying *"stop"*, *"volume down"*, or giving a new command.

- [ ] **1.2. Zero-Gap Music Streaming & DJ Auto-Next**
  - Direct audio streaming via `mpg123` with 1024KB pre-buffering.
  - When 20 seconds remain on a song, queries `/device/:slug/music/recommend` and pre-buffers the next track for seamless, gapless playback.

- [ ] **1.3. Multi-Source Streaming (YouTube & Spotify Integration)**
  - Direct keyword routing: *"Play Believer on YouTube"* or *"Play Starboy on Spotify"*.
  - Instant stream resolution without local video transcoding.

- [ ] **1.4. Bluetooth Speaker Ultrasonic Keep-Alive**
  - Sends a silent, imperceptible 1ms pulse every 3 minutes to prevent Bluetooth speakers from entering auto-sleep or deep standby.

- [ ] **1.5. Local Earcon Soundboard (Instant Micro-Feedback)**
  - Sub-millisecond local audio cues loaded in RAM:
    - *Wake Chime*: Confirms microphone is listening.
    - *Thinking Pulse*: Subtle pulse while cloud tasks generate.
    - *Success Ding*: On alarm set, light toggled, or task completed.
    - *Error Tone*: When Wi-Fi or backend is unreachable.

- [ ] **1.6. Mobile / Web Audio Handoff**
  - Say *"Send audio to phone"* or tap a button on the Rexycore mobile dashboard to transfer active music/podcasts from the Pi to your phone seamlessly.

## ⚡ 2. Instant Local Hardware Controls (Zero Latency, <1ms)

- [ ] **2.1. Instant Hardware Volume Mixer**
  - Local ALSA `amixer` hardware volume adjustments:
    - *"Volume up"* / *"Volume down"* / *"Make it louder"* / *"Turn it down"*
    - Absolute levels: *"Set volume to 70%"*
    - Direct mute/unmute: *"Mute"* / *"Unmute"*

- [ ] **2.2. Fast Playback Controls**
  - Zero-latency local execution: *"Pause"*, *"Resume"*, *"Stop"*, *"Shut up"*, *"Silence"*.

- [ ] **2.3. System Self-Healing & Hardware Power**
  - Strict `hci1` adapter enforcement (reboots if missing to recover USB bus).
  - Voice system actions: *"Restart system"*, *"Reboot Pi"*, *"Power off"*.

- [ ] **2.4. Offline Voice Diagnostic Self-Check**
  - Say *"Run diagnostics"* or *"Check system"*.
  - Assistant speaks: *"All systems normal. Speaker connected with 92% link quality. Wi-Fi strong at -50 dBm. CPU temperature 44°C. RAM usage 28 MB."*

## ⏰ 3. Alarms, Timers, Routines & Education Suite

- [ ] **3.1. Two-Way Cloud Alarms & Recurring Schedules**
  - Natural alarm commands: *"Wake me up tomorrow at 7:00 AM"* or *"Set alarm for 6:30 AM every weekday"*.
  - Synced two-way with the mobile app (`/device/:slug/sync_alarms`).
  - Alarm sound accompanied by a personalized motivational morning wake-up greeting generated by Gemini.

- [ ] **3.2. Classroom & Study Period Bell**
  - Built-in `period_bell` support matching school/study timetables with custom chime intervals.

- [ ] **3.3. Pomodoro Study Timer**
  - *"Start a 25-minute study session"*:
    - Plays soft background focus sound $\rightarrow$ rings the period bell at 25 mins $\rightarrow$ announces 5-minute break $\rightarrow$ re-rings to resume.

- [ ] **3.4. Teacher & Student AI Cloud Generative Tools**
  - Generates materials in the background on the cloud and speaks confirmation:
    - *"Generate an exam paper on Chapter 5 Physics"* (`exam_paper`)
    - *"Create a grading sheet for Grade 10 Math"* (`grading_sheet`)
    - *"Make a class planner for next month"* (`class_planner`)
    - *"Write study notes for Biology"* (`note` / `docx`)

- [ ] **3.5. Interactive Voice Quiz / Flashcard Mode**
  - *"Quiz me on History Chapter 3"*:
    - Assistant asks Question 1 $\rightarrow$ listens to spoken answer $\rightarrow$ evaluates and gives immediate voice feedback $\rightarrow$ moves to Question 2.

## 🧠 4. Conversational Intelligence & Smart Assistant (Alexa-Grade)

- [ ] **4.1. Conversational Context Stacking (Multi-Turn Follow-Ups)**
  - Local 3-turn memory stack so the user can speak naturally without repeating context:
    - User: *"What's the weather in Mumbai?"* $\rightarrow$ Assistant answers.
    - User: *"Will it rain there tomorrow?"* $\rightarrow$ Automatically resolves "there" to Mumbai.
    - User: *"Play something like this"* $\rightarrow$ Recommends music related to current track.

- [ ] **4.2. "Start My Day" / Good Morning Routine Briefing**
  - Trigger: *"Good morning"* or *"Start my day"*.
  - Reads: Custom greeting (*"Radhe Radhe!"*) + Current weather & forecast + Top 2 news headlines + Today's active alarms/schedule + Starts favorite morning playlist.

- [ ] **4.3. Voice Brain-Dump & Quick Notes to Dashboard**
  - *"Note this down: Submit assignment by 5 PM tomorrow"*.
  - Saves directly to your Rexycore cloud dashboard with tags (Tasks / Study / General).

- [ ] **4.4. Adaptive Night / Whisper Mode**
  - Automatically activates late at night (11 PM – 6 AM) or on quiet ambient speech:
  - Drops TTS volume to a gentle 25% with soft phrasing to avoid waking others.

## 📱 5. Phone & IoT Integration

- [ ] **5.1. "Find My Phone / Ring My Device"**
  - Voice trigger: *"Hey RK, where is my phone?"* or *"Ring my phone"*.
  - Dispatches high-priority cloud push to override silent mode on paired phone and ring at max volume.

- [ ] **5.2. Remote Command Polling (Mobile App Control)**
  - Mobile app or web dashboard can command the Pi remotely:
    - Trigger voice announcements.
    - Ring alarm remotely.
    - Remotely adjust volume or pause music.

- [ ] **5.3. Intercom & Voice Broadcast**
  - From Phone: Send a voice or text announcement to play on the Pi speaker (*"Dinner is ready!"*).
  - From Pi: *"Announce that I am busy studying"* $\rightarrow$ sends notification to family devices.

- [ ] **5.4. Emergency SOS Siren & Contact Alert**
  - Trigger: *"Emergency"*, *"Help me"*, or *"Alert"*.
  - Immediately pulses loud siren through the speaker and sends an urgent push/SMS alert to emergency contacts.

- [ ] **5.5. Auto-Recovery Wi-Fi Hotspot**
  - If Wi-Fi is lost for >90 seconds, speaks: *"I lost Wi-Fi connection. Connect to 'RK-Setup' to reconfigure"*, and temporarily exposes BLE/AP for easy phone pairing without needing SSH.

## 🛠️ 6. Performance & Architecture Constraints

- [x] **Strict Bluetooth Pinning**: Adapter `hci1` ONLY. Reboots if missing.
- [x] **Speaker Lock**: Default MAC `D0:78:1D:4F:F4:1E` with PulseAudio `a2dp_sink` priority.
- [x] **Ultra-Light Footprint**: Resident memory budget under **35MB RAM** (leaving 450MB+ free for OS and audio buffering).
- [x] **No Local Heavy ML**: Zero `scikit-learn`, zero local heavy Gemini SDKs. Cloud handles generative tasks; Pi handles fast routing and audio output.
